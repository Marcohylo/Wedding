Our Story

We met in Melbourne through a dating app, a moment we still fondly debate. Marco, an Australian-born Melburnian, and Kaya, who had recently moved from Hong Kong, quickly found a connection that felt natural and effortless. From that first meeting, our journey together began.

  • First We Meet

    May

    We met through Hinge (or Bumble, according to Kaya). From the first glance, Marco was drawn to Kaya’s love for Disney and her roots in Hong Kong. What began as a simple introduction soon grew into an effortless connection between two people from different worlds.

  • First Date

    June

    Our first date was a relaxed coffee in a charming café, a place that no longer exists but remains special to us. It was also the first time Marco met one of the most important parts of Kaya’s world, her dog, Porsche, who you may spot running across this website from time to time.

  • In A Relationship

    October

    Time has flown since we became a couple, and together we have shared many adventures across Australia. Each experience has strengthened our bond, and as we look ahead, we are excited for all the moments and journeys still to come.

How to Get There

Fly to Nadi (NAN)

Most guests will arrive at Nadi International Airport on direct flights from Australia.

Taxi or Shuttle to Port Denarau

Approx 20–25 minutes from the airport.

Taxi: FJD $40–$60 (~AUD $28–$42)

Free shuttle: If you're staying at Musket Cove, they provide a free shuttle bus between Nadi Airport ↔ Port Denarau when you book the Malolo Cat ferry.

Ferry to Musket Cove

Malolo Cat ferry from Port Denarau.

Adult: FJD $90 (~AUD $63) one-way

Child: FJD $45 (~AUD $32) one-way

Approx 50 mins, multiple departures daily.

Book Ferry Tickets

Arrive in Paradise

The ferry drops you right at the Musket Cove marina. Check in, relax and enjoy island life.
